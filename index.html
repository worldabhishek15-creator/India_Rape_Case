Institutional response

What the supplied review documents.

Investigation & accountability

  • FIR registration and arrests appear repeatedly in the reviewed reports.
  • Medical examinations, forensic/crime-scene work and CCTV/technical investigation are recurring investigative steps.
  • The April 2026 Delhi case later reached chargesheet stage, with extensive CCTV and forensic work reported.
  • In Amroha, two UP Police personnel were reported suspended and departmental dismissal proceedings were initiated.

Oversight & survivor support

  • NHRC repeatedly sought investigation and compensation-status reports.
  • NCW sought an Action Taken Report in the Jamia Nagar case covering arrests, forensic work, survivor safety, counselling and rehabilitation.
  • The review records counselling/support, medical care and rehabilitation-related institutional requests in several entries.

Legal framework

The supplied briefing records the Ministry of Home Affairs' March 2026 description of BNS provisions concerning offences against women and children, including specified strict punishments. It also notes that POCSO provisions are commonly invoked alongside BNS provisions for children.

Data limitations

  • Not a complete national FIR census.
  • Media visibility is uneven.
  • Reporting date and incident date may differ.
  • Official actions can become public later.
  • Do not use this register to calculate India's rape rate.
